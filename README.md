**Belle Food**

A food kitchen and grocery mart in one app. Order for delivery or pickup across Lagos, with an admin panel and a customer rewards program.

**Features**

- Two stores in one app: Meals (kitchen) and Mart (groceries)
- Cart, checkout, delivery or pickup with per-area fees
- Pay by bank transfer, Paystack, or points
- Live order tracking with status updates
- Points and referral rewards, spendable on meals and delivery
- Admin panel for products, categories, orders, customers and settings
- Order alerts with sound and device notifications
- Installable on phone and desktop

**Tech stack**

- Next.js, React, TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Supabase
- Cloudinary
- Paystack
